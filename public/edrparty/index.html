COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked.

What is your delivery fee policy? +

Orders of $349+ receive FREE local delivery within 15 miles. For orders under $349, standard delivery is $49 (0–15 miles), $69 (16–25 miles), or $89+ for 26–35 miles.

What is your weather & cancellation policy? +

If rain or winds exceeding 15mph are forecast, you can cancel by 7:00 AM on your event date for a full refund of your deposit. Safety is our priority.

What cities do you serve? +

We deliver to Austin, Cedar Park, Leander, Liberty Hill, Del Valle, Jarrell, Salado, Florence, and surrounding Central Texas communities. Enter your ZIP code in our availability bar to verify delivery.
